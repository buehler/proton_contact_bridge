Your contacts, clearly organised.

The people you want to keep in touch with, in one focused place. KinCrypt is a free contact manager that connects directly to Proton.

Explore featuresHow your data is handled

A little more order.
A lot less searching.

Keep your attention on your contacts. Names, details, favorites, and groups belong together, with a clear path from finding someone to updating their information.

People, with the details that matter

Browse your contacts and open a clear view of their phone numbers, email addresses, and other details. Create contacts and edit information in one place.

Your favorites, close at hand

Mark the people you reach for most as favorites, so they are easy to find again.

Groups that make sense to you

Organise contacts into groups and see who belongs together. Keep family, friends, and other circles easy to navigate.

Connected directly to Proton

Bring your Proton contacts into KinCrypt and synchronize changes in both directions. Contacts are stored locally, with synchronization status showing when changes are still waiting to reach Proton.

Your address book.
A direct connection.

KinCrypt connects to Proton without a KinCrypt server in between. There is no app analytics or automatic log upload.

Session tokens stay in your device’s secure storage. Your password is used for SRP authentication, the protocol also used by Proton, and is never saved persistently.
